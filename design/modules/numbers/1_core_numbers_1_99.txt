### **1b. Aural to Numeral Recognition (Type 1b)**

#### **Listening to Numeral Recognition**

* **Prompt:** Play audio "shí qī".
    * **Task:** Type `17`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "sān shí wǔ".
    * **Task:** Type `35`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "wǔ shí".
    * **Task:** Type `50`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "bā shí liù".
    * **Task:** Type `86`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "jiǔ shí jiǔ".
    * **Task:** Type `99`.
