### **Part 2: Hundreds & Thousands**

* **Type 1a (Character & Word Recognition):**
    * **Prompt:** Show character `百`. **Task:** Provide pinyin ("bǎi") and meaning ("hundred").
    * **Prompt:** Show character `千`. **Task:** Provide pinyin ("qiān") and meaning ("thousand").

* **Type 2a (Fill-in-the-Blank):**
    * **Prompt:** "105 is written as 一百 ___ 五". **Task:** Type `零`.
    * **Prompt:** "The number 6,018 is written as 六千 ___ 十八". **Task:** Type `零`.

* **Type 3a (English-to-Chinese Translation):**
    * **Prompt:** "742". **Task:** Type `七百四十二`.
    * **Prompt:** "2,500". **Task:** Type `两千五百`.

---
### **Part 3: Large Numbers (The Ten Thousand System)**

* **Type 1a (Character & Word Recognition):**
    * **Prompt:** Show character `万`. **Task:** Provide pinyin ("wàn") and meaning ("ten thousand").
    * **Prompt:** Show character `亿`. **Task:** Provide pinyin ("yì") and meaning ("hundred million").

* **Type 3a (English-to-Chinese Translation):**
    * **Prompt:** "10,000". **Task:** Type `一万`.
    * **Prompt:** "350,000". **Task:** Type `三十五万`.
    * **Prompt:** "1,250,000". **Task:** Type `一百二十五万`.

* **Type 4a (Comprehension Question):**
    * **Prompt:** Play audio "五万八千 (wǔwàn bāqiān)".
    * **Task:** Ask question: "Which number is this?"
        * A) 5,800
        * B) 58,000
        * C) 580,000

### **Part 4: Special Cases**

* **Type 1a (Character & Word Recognition):**
    * **Prompt:** Show character `点`. **Task:** Provide pinyin ("diǎn") and meaning ("point").
    * **Prompt:** Show character `负`. **Task:** Provide pinyin ("fù") and meaning ("negative").

* **Type 3a (English-to-Chinese Translation):**
    * **Prompt:** "9.81". **Task:** Type `九点八一`.
    * **Prompt:** "-15". **Task:** Type `负十五`.

---
### **Part 5: Key Concepts & Nuances**

* **Type 2a (Fill-in-the-Blank):**
    * **Prompt:** Show context: "我有 ___ 个苹果。(I have two apples.)".
    * **Task:** Type the correct character for "two": `两`.

* **Type 4b (Dialogue Response):**
    * **Prompt:** A cashier says the total is "十二块 (shí'èr kuài)". The app asks: "How would you say 'second floor'?"
    * **Task:** Type `二楼`. (This tests that the user knows `èr` is for ordinals).

* **Type 3a (English-to-Chinese Translation):**
    * **Prompt:** "Year 2025".
    * **Task:** Type `二〇二五`. (This tests the preferred informal character for years).
