### **Exercises for Module 2: Numbers, Part 2 (Hundreds & Thousands)**

This document details the exercises for learning numbers from 100 to 9,999.

---
### **1. Foundations: Reading & Recognition (Type 1a)**

* **Prompt:** Show character `百`.
    * **Task:** Provide pinyin ("bǎi") and meaning ("hundred").

* **Prompt:** Show character `千`.
    * **Task:** Provide pinyin ("qiān") and meaning ("thousand").

* **Prompt:** Show character `两`.
    * **Task:** Provide pinyin ("liǎng") and meaning ("2, used for counting").

* **Prompt:** Show word `一百`.
    * **Task:** Provide spoken pinyin ("yìbǎi") and meaning ("100"). This tests the tone change rule for `一`.

* **Prompt:** Show word `一千`.
    * **Task:** Provide spoken pinyin ("yìqiān") and meaning ("1,000"). This tests the tone change rule for `一`.

---
### **2. Structure: Grammar & Contextual Recall (Type 2a)**

* **Prompt:** "105 is written as 一百 ___ 五".
    * **Task:** Type `零`.

* **Prompt:** "The number 6,018 is written as 六千 ___ 十八".
    * **Task:** Type `零`.

* **Prompt:** "We have two hundred people: 我们有 ___ 百人。"
    * **Task:** Type `两`.

---
### **3. Production & Synthesis (Types 3a & 3b)**

#### **English-to-Chinese Translation (Typing)**

* **Prompt:** "100".
    * **Task:** Type `一百`.

* **Prompt:** "300".
    * **Task:** Type `三百`.

* **Prompt:** "742".
    * **Task:** Type `七百四十二`.

* **Prompt:** "1,000".
    * **Task:** Type `一千`.

* **Prompt:** "2,000".
    * **Task:** Type `两千`.

* **Prompt:** "8,888".
    * **Task:** Type `八千八百八十八`.

* **Prompt:** "5,009".
    * **Task:** Type `五千零九`.

#### **Listening Dictation to Handwriting**

* **Prompt:** Play audio "bǎi".
    * **Task:** Handwrite `百`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "qiān".
    * **Task:** Handwrite `千`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "yībǎi líng wǔ".
    * **Task:** Handwrite `一百零五`.

---
### **4. Mastery Challenge: Listening Comprehension (Type 4a)**

* **Prompt:** Play audio "七百四十二 (qībǎi sìshí'èr)".
    * **Task:** Type the number using digits: `742`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "两千五百 (liǎngqiān wǔbǎi)".
    * **Task:** Type the number using digits: `2500`.

* **Prompt:** Play audio "九千零一 (jiǔqiān líng yī)".
    * **Task:** Type the number using digits: `9001`.
